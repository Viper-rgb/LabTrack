# LabTrack
is a digital laboratory management system designed to simplify and streamline the daily operations of medical diagnostic laboratories. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Features

Sample Registration: Register patient samples and assign unique sample identification numbers or QR codes.

1.Test Tracking: Monitor the progress of diagnostic tests through different stages, including sample collection, testing, review, and completion.

2.Laboratory Workflows: Allow technicians to process samples and enter results, while authorized pathologists review and approve reports.

3.Report Generation: Generate downloadable PDF reports containing patient details, diagnostic test results, and approval information.

4.Dashboard and Analytics: Display sample counts, pending tests, completed tests, and reports awaiting approval.

5.Search and Date Filters: Search records and filter laboratory activities by date, sample ID, user, or test status.

6.Activity Logs: Maintain a history of important actions to support traceability and accountability.

7.Role-Based Access: Provide appropriate permissions and interfaces for administrators, laboratory technicians, and pathologists.
